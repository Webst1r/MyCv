Data analysis · Apr 2026 – Present

Body Measurements Analysis

Uncovering human body proportion patterns

Analysed 2,018 subjects across 15 body measurements to identify key trends in human proportions and body composition.

  • Strong bilateral symmetry: 0.929 correlation between arm and leg length
  • Three distinct body shapes: 49% Proportional, 38% Pear, 13% Apple
  • Waist circumference varies 42% between extreme groups, the most variable measurement
  • Torso measurements move together (0.927 chest–waist correlation)
  • Mapped 15 common body profiles, from “Short + Pear + Low Muscle” (most common, 12.5%) to “Tall + Apple + High Muscle” (most extreme)

Tools: SQL for data analysis, statistical correlation analysis, percentile-based segmentation

Applications: apparel sizing, ergonomic design, health assessment, fitness benchmarking
